Class Representative (CR) Protocols
Discipline Protocol
Official Dispersal Protocol (PGP ERP 2026-28)
  1. 1
    In-Class Queue Formation: Ensure all students stand in a single-file line inside the classroom first; do not open the door or depart until the entire class is fully lined up and ready.
  2. 2
    Inter-Section CR Coordination: CRs must actively communicate with the CR of the adjacent section before initiating class departure to prevent overlapping releases.
  3. 3
    Bypass on Delay: If the section scheduled to exit first is not ready, the succeeding section may proceed ahead after immediately informing the CR of the delayed section.
  4. 4
    Left-Side Single-File Transit: Instruct all students to move strictly along the left side of the corridor and staircases, maintaining single-file order with zero double-filing or overtaking.
  5. 5
    Strict Silence in Transit: Enforce complete silence while moving through corridors and staircases. Talking and shouting are strictly prohibited.
  6. 6
    Spacing & Posture Safety: Ensure students maintain adequate physical distance from the person ahead. Hands must be kept out of pockets at all times while moving, especially on stairs.
  7. 7
    Classroom Utilities & Security: Turn off all fans, lights, and projectors, and securely close the classroom door before the last student departs.
  8. 8
    Punctuality Enforcement: Late comers not allowed. Ensure all students report back to the classroom strictly before the session resumes.
